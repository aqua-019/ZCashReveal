Beware: what the proof could not prove

Fourteen entries. Two of them are unlimited-counterfeiting windows totalling about six of the chain's 9.8 years, and neither can be cleared after the fact — a zero-knowledge pool hides the crime as well as it hides the coins. The rest are the side-channels, the spam, the skipped proofs, the turnstile that could be switched off, and the audits that looked straight past all of it.

The Orchard soundness flaw, in full

CVE-2026-54496 · GHSA-ww9q-8r59-xv46 · CVSS 9.3 · window 31 May 2022 → 1 Jun 2026 (4 years, 1 day)
root cause · halo2_gadgets/src/ecc/chip/mul/incomplete.rs · L309–310
// the incomplete double-and-add loop kept the base constant across rows via q_mul_2,
// but never tied it to the real base point g_d — the base was free.
- region.assign_advice(|| "x_p", self.double_and_add.x_p, row + offset, || x_p)?;
- region.assign_advice(|| "y_p", self.y_p,                 row + offset, || y_p)?;
+ if row == 0 {
+     region.copy_advice(|| "base", self.base, 0, self.double_and_add.x_p, row)?;
+ }
// consequence: pk_d = [ivk]·g_d was never enforced → forged nullifier keys → the same note
// spendable repeatedly under fresh nullifiers. Consensus only rejects *repeated* nullifiers.

ZIP 209 forbids a pool balance going negative, so the 21M cap was never at risk at the consensus layer. Inside the pool, forged notes are indistinguishable from real ones: a counterfeiter would have drained other users' legitimate ZEC. “Unlimited counterfeit” and “supply was safe” are both true at different layers.

Shielded → bounded

An address is never written to the chain. Without a key we show what the pool publishes around a transaction: anchor-bounded candidate sets, spent-count subtraction, time and amount echoes, fee-derived action counts, wallet fingerprints — each with its assumption and its cost.

Viewing key → exact, yours

Trial-decrypt every output with your incoming key, derive nullifiers with your full key, read your own outgoing ciphertexts — in the browser, never uploaded. The only way a shielded balance appears on this site.

1

Transparent side — exact, plus clustering

deterministic · the Bitcoin toolkit applies unchanged
1.1 balance & history

bal(a) = Σu ∈ UTXO(a) v(u). History = every tx with an input or output script paying a. Fees = Σin − Σout − valueBalance terms; the ZIP 317 conventional fee implies the logical-action count L = fee / 5,000.

1.2 common-input ownership

All transparent inputs of one tx are spent by one key-holder → union-find over input scripts → clusters C(a). Exceptions flagged: coinjoin-like shapes (rare on Zcash) and P2SH multisigs — the ZIP 271 lockbox is a cluster of signers, not one owner.

1.3 change detection

Two-output tx, one fresh same-type address, the other round or reused → the fresh one is change with probability pchange, calibrated on Zcash and printed. Soft cluster membership, never fact.

1.4 exchange shapes · 1.5 consensus labels

One large input → payout + change back to the same address (t1PKBiv7… on 24 Dec 2025: 120,552.69 → 29,999.99 + 90,552.70) and many-to-one sweeps justify “exchange hot wallet” (high) but not which exchange. Labels with certainty exist only in consensus: ZIP 271 multisig, ZIP 1014/1015/1016 streams, the Founders' Reward set, TEX receivers (ZIP 320). Precedence: consensus › owner filing › exchange confirmation › analyst › behaviour.

Combining estimators — posterior, entropy, claim

the number a reader sees is Neff, never a name
the posterior
wj ∝ Lamount(Y | Xj) · Ltime(h − hj) · Lfp(T, Tj) · Lstruct(Tj)
pj = wj / Σ w
H = −Σj pj log₂ pj        Neff = 2H        claim = level(Neff)

Lamount = 1 for exact, exp(−(|Xj−Y|/Xj)/ε) for relative; Ltime is the half-life kernel; Lfp 1.0 when fingerprints agree, 0.5 when they disagree; Lstruct down-weights candidates already consumed by a HIGH link (one-to-one, greedy by weight). The UI prints counts before/after each filter, the top-3 candidates with pj, Neff, the claim chip, and the assumption sentences (W, τ, ε, fingerprint-table version).

flow estimate over hops · “taint”

From a transparent address, follow value through k ≤ 3 boundary crossings multiplying pj along paths; draw edges with opacity ∝ weight; cut at p < 0.02; show the mass unresolved in the pool as a first-class residual bar — because it is the honest answer most of the time.

what this is not

None of it identifies a person. It produces bounded, reproducible estimates from public data, with every assumption visible and every claim capped by the claim level.

06 · FLOWS  —  exchange inflows & the insider question · research date 22 Aug 2026 · chain state 3,456,938

What the chain shows vs. what is claimed

Every claim on this page is split four ways: (a) what the chain shows — reproducible from public block data; (b) who labelled it and how; (c) inference vs. fact; (d) confidence. No identity is asserted from chain data alone. Where an entity name sits beside an address it is someone else's label, attributed, with the confidence reflecting their evidence.

in one line

Custody is documented; addresses are not. The largest unshielding of the cycle mostly never reached an exchange. The dev fund is 99.28% untouched and untraceable by design. Nobody has produced on-chain evidence of insider selling — and the allegations in circulation cite no addresses, no transactions, and no supply audit.

49,999.97 ZEC left t1PKBiv7… on 24–25 Dec 2025 to a fresh address; that address shielded its whole balance on 2 Jan at 18:01:43; 50,000.5541 ZEC emerged 52 minutes later (Δ 0.4059) and returned to the same wallet. Net new inflow ≤ ~24,001 ZEC. medium-high — the pool breaks the link by design.

78,750 ZEC minted at NU6.1 (block 3,146,400) as 10 × 7,875 to the ZIP 271 2-of-3 multisig (ZF · ECC · Shielded Labs). 566.5907 ZEC (0.72%) has net left — into shielded outputs, as the ZIP mandates. Disbursement is untraceable to any exchange after the first hop: a design property, not a cover-up.

1.5 · custody is documented, addresses are not

Grayscale Zcash Trust → Coinbase Custody Trust Company (388,673.68 ZEC at 30 Jun 2026). Cypherpunk Technologies → Gemini (323,394.38 ZEC; from its own 10-Q). Neither publishes addresses or proof of reserves. No public rich list labels any exchange or custodian on Zcash.

Grayscale's Sponsor's Fee, paid in kind: 4,848.65 ZEC in H1 2026, 4,883.12 in H1 2025 — ≈ 9,700 ZEC/yr (~$7.9M) withdrawn from the Trust to the Sponsor. Whether the Sponsor sells it is not disclosed.
