Claim 3

Two windows in Zcash's history cannot be proven sound, and 731,462 ZEC still sits inside the pools they touched.

Evidence 10

Orchard Action circuit soundness — missing copy constraint

A single missing equality constraint in the halo2 scalar-multiplication gadget left the Orchard Action circuit's key-to-nullifier binding unenforced for four years, letting a malicious prover forge nullifiers and spend authorisations.

CVSS 9.3 critical CVE-2026-54496 window 4y 0m confirmed · 6 primary sources verified 15 Jun 2026
Timeline7 events, 31 May 2022 – 15 Jun 2026

Live at NU5/Orchard activation, block 1,687,104, 31 May 2022. Found 29 May 2026 by Taylor Hornby using an AI-assisted auditing framework. Confirmed 30 May. Emergency soft fork at block 3,363,426 disabled Orchard network-wide. NU6.2 shipped the corrected circuit at block 3,364,600 on 3 June. Disclosed 4 June; advisory 15 June.

Root cause1 file, 3 lines

halo2_gadgets/src/ecc/chip/mul/incomplete.rs — the gadget omitted the equality constraint binding the accumulated scalar to the witnessed key, so the prover was free to choose them independently.
